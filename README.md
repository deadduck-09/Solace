# Solace 🌿

**A calm, minimal space for thoughtful reflection, journaling, and tracking personal growth.**

Solace is designed to provide a quiet, distraction-free environment to capture your daily thoughts, track your mood, and reflect on your personal journey. Built with clean aesthetics and privacy in mind, it serves as your personal digital sanctuary away from the noise of social media.

## 🌟 Overview

Life gets noisy. Solace gives you a quiet corner of the digital world to slow down, put your thoughts into words, and make sense of your days.

Whether you're documenting everyday moments, processing your thoughts, or looking back at how far you've come, Solace helps you build a meaningful journaling habit in a simple, personal space.

## ✨ Features

- 📝 **Distraction-Free Editor** — A minimalist writing experience designed to keep your attention on your thoughts, not the interface.
- 🔒 **Privacy-First** — Designed with privacy in mind, featuring encrypted local storage to help keep your personal reflections secure.
- 📊 **Mood & Growth Tracking** — Discover patterns in your mood and reflect on your emotional journey over time.
- 🏷️ **Tagging & Organization** — Organize entries by themes, events, feelings, or anything that matters to you.
- 🌙 **Dark & Light Modes** — Switch between themes to match your environment and preferences.
- 📅 **Daily Journaling** — Build a consistent habit of documenting your thoughts and experiences.

## 🚀 Quick Start

### Prerequisites

Make sure you have the following installed:

- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) (v18 or higher)
- npm (included with Node.js) or Yarn
- Git

### Installation

**1. Clone the repository**

```bash
git clone https://github.com/deadduck_09/solace.git
cd solace
```

**2. Install dependencies**

Using npm:

```bash
npm install
```

Or using Yarn:

```bash
yarn install
```

**3. Start the development server**

```bash
npm run dev
```

**4. Open Solace**

Visit [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser to explore the application.

> **Note:** The development URL and available features depend on the project's actual configuration.

## 🔐 Privacy

Your journal is personal. Solace aims to keep your reflections private and your data under your control.

The goal is to prioritize local data storage, minimize unnecessary data collection, and provide a secure space for personal writing.

_Note: Everything is stored locally in your browser._
